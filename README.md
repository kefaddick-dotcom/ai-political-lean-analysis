# ai-political-lean-analysis - a portfolio project
This is a master page of data, scripts and notebooks pertaining to projects involving political lean in frontier LLMs. 
I aim to fill this page with every step of the ongoing In Claude We Trust project to outline the methodological innovations and experimental findings that surface throughout the process. 
This page will function as a journal of my experience, my hardships, my successes, and most importantly my process in learning and exploring how to extract and work with such rich data.
My goal is to eventually curate a portfolio of statistical models, analytical methods, and impactful finding to show my skills and their development throughout this project.
